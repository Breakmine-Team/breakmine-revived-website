What is Breakmine?

Breakmine is an open-source voxel sandbox game, made in JavaScript. Explore infinite worlds, build whatever you imagine, and customize your experience with mods.

Developed by SpinningCubes, Paradoxism, Marw-Programmer, and LowQualityCoding.
